## ★ 표정·동작 레퍼토리 (밋밋한 '살랑' 금지 — 컷마다 다른 연기)
**레오 (orange tabby cat):**
- 꼬리: 평상시 느린 저음 스월 → ★**놀라면 갑자기 두껍게 부풀며 병솔(고슴도치)처럼 뻣뻣이 뻗침** (서프라이즈 시그니처).
- 수염: 호기심=앞으로 쏠림/떨림, 긴장=뒤로 눕힘.
- 귀: 소리 쪽으로 쫑긋/기울임 — 한쪽만 움직여도 OK(특히 전화는 한쪽 귀에 있으니 자연스러움). ★요점은 **어색하게/뻣뻣하게/과장되게 하지 말 것**, 실제 고양이처럼 자연스럽게. 눈: 느린 롱잉 깜빡, **놀람=동공 확 커짐**. 고개 천천히 돌려 시선 이동.

**랴니 (black French bulldog, ★NO TAIL 항상 명시):**
- **고개 갸웃(head tilt)** · **입 벌림**(멍/야옹 흉내) · **귀 쫑긋** · **윙크** · **어깨 으쓱(shoulder shrug)** · 흰 블레이즈 실룩 · 느린 깜빡.

**카메라:** push-in / pull-back / slow arc·orbit / dolly / rack-focus + **한 컷 안 뷰 체인지**(와이드→클로즈·각도 전환)로 생동감.
**규칙(sora2_motion_lessons):** 고유명사 금지(breed/color로), 랴니 "no tail" 명시, 한 동작에 수식어 1개(동작은 "then"으로 순차 체인 OK), warp/morph 동사 금지, 고양이 꼬리 모션 필수.

## 컷별 모션 프롬프트 (13컷) — 순차 비트로 디테일
1. **거리 WIDE** — camera glides slowly in down the wet cobblestone toward the glowing bar; fine rain falls, the hanging bar sign sways, the neon flickers, a puddle ripples. (no pet)
2. **서빙·유니폼** — gentle drift-in. The small no-tail French bulldog sits on the diner stool; she tilts her head to one side, then her ears perk up, then a slow blink; steam curls off the plate; a ceiling fan turns slowly behind.
3. **카운터 투샷** — slow push-in across the counter. The orange tabby cat turns his eyes sideways toward her, whiskers twitch forward, the tail-tip flicks once; behind the counter the no-tail French bulldog perks her ears and gives a small shoulder shrug, playing it cool.
4. **레오 롱잉 CU** — slow creep-in to a tight close-up. The orange tabby cat holds a wistful look, gives one slow longing blink, whiskers quiver, both ears tip back softly together, the tail sways in a low slow arc; his chest lifts in a quiet sigh.
5. **웜 두샷** — camera holds, breathing. A shy beat: the no-tail French bulldog tilts her head then gives a bashful shoulder shrug; the orange tabby cat flicks an ear and leans a touch closer; both glance away, then back.
6. **파티 레오 CU** — slow orbit, green and red light pulsing across his face. The orange tabby cat slowly turns his head to find her across the crowd (view change), his pupils dilate wide, whiskers flare; a yearning stillness, tail-tip twitching.
7. **파티 랴니 시퀸** — slow arc around her, sequins sparking green/red. The no-tail French bulldog tilts her head, her ears droop then perk, her mouth opens in a small wistful sigh, then a soft shoulder shrug; the crowd sways in soft focus.
8. **피아노 WIDE** — slow dolly through the dark hall toward the piano. The orange tabby cat sways gently with the music as he plays, head dipping, tail draped over the bench swaying; candle flames flicker, dust floats in the amber beam.
9. **발 인서트** — macro, shallow focus. The cat's striped paws rise and press the keys in slow succession; a hammer glints inside the piano; warm bokeh.
10. **듣기·반다나** — slow push-in. The no-tail French bulldog's ears perk sharply toward the music, she tilts her head curiously, her eyes soften, then she gives a slow knowing wink; candle bokeh breathes behind.
11. **★전화 레오 ECU (KILLPOINT — 제일 길게)** — slow push-in, very tight. BEAT 1 (calm): the orange tabby cat holds the receiver to one ear, the near ear tilting naturally toward it to listen (a soft, natural cat movement — never stiff or exaggerated), whiskers still. BEAT 2 (surprise HITS): his eyes fly wide and pupils blow huge, both ears shoot straight up, whiskers flare forward, his mouth parts in a tiny gasp — and ★his tail suddenly puffs up thick and bristles stiff like a bottlebrush. BEAT 3 (melt): a trembling overwhelmed blink, a shaky breath. Let the surprise land and linger.
12. **★전화 랴니 ECU (KILLPOINT — 길게)** — slow push-in, warm bokeh. The no-tail French bulldog lifts the receiver with a nervous paw, ears half-back with nerves; she tilts her head and her mouth opens as she speaks, the white blaze crinkling; her shoulders give a small shy shrug; then, gathering courage, her ears perk and she gives a hopeful shy wink.
13. **엔딩·유니폼** — slow pull-back from the glass. The no-tail French bulldog presses one paw flat on the rain-streaked window, watching him go; her ears droop, a slow blink, then a small hopeful perk of the ears and a lift of the chin; rain runs down the pane, the warm bar glows behind; the frame settles and fades.
